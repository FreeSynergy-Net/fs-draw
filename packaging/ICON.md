# Icon

`net.freesynergy.Draw.png` (256x256) is the FreeSynergy Draw icon: our own drawing (source `fs-draw.svg` of the FreeSynergy icon
set, `.scratch/katalog-plan/icons-k2`), licence MIT, SPDX-License-Identifier: MIT. It is also compiled into the window of the
frontend `simple` (`crates/fs-draw-simple/src/surface.rs`, `include_bytes!`).
